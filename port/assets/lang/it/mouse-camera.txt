# The mouse camera (port/settings/features/view.cpp): an English line, then its translation, then a blank line.

Mouse camera
Telecamera col mouse

With the free camera on, the mouse turns it too while Mario walks, and the window holds the cursor then; menus and scenes give it back. A middle click centres the camera.
Con la telecamera libera attiva, anche il mouse la gira mentre Mario cammina, e intanto la finestra trattiene il puntatore; menu e scene lo restituiscono. Un clic col tasto centrale ricentra la telecamera.

Mouse camera speed
Velocità della telecamera col mouse

How far the camera turns for a move of the mouse.
Quanto gira la telecamera per un movimento del mouse.

Invert mouse camera
Inverti la telecamera col mouse

Moving the mouse up moves the camera out and higher.
Mouse in alto: la telecamera si allontana e si alza.
