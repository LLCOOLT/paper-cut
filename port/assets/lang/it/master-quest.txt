A line each; # starts a note.\nname <the set's name>\nfight <area> <lead> = <enemy> <enemy> ...: in that area (hei, gon, win...; jon is the Pit), a fight led by <lead> becomes one of its lines (1-5 enemies, only the area's own).\nenemy <area or *> <enemy> <changes>: hp+N, hp*P (percent), atk+N, hit=<status>:<chance>:<turns>[:<strength>] (sleep stop dizzy poison confuse burn freeze tiny attack_down defense_down slow), start=<status>:<turns>[:<strength>] (dodgy huge attack_up defense_up charge invisible fast payback hold_fast hp_regen electric), element=fire|ice|explosion|electric, pierce.
Una riga ciascuna; # inizia una nota.\nname <nome del set>\nfight <zona> <capofila> = <nemico> <nemico> ...: in quella zona (hei, gon, win...; jon è il Pozzo), una battaglia guidata da <capofila> diventa una delle sue righe (1-5 nemici, solo quelli della zona).\nenemy <zona o *> <nemico> <modifiche>: hp+N, hp*P (percento), atk+N, hit=<stato>:<probabilità>:<turni>[:<forza>] (sleep stop dizzy poison confuse burn freeze tiny attack_down defense_down slow), start=<stato>:<turni>[:<forza>] (dodgy huge attack_up defense_up charge invisible fast payback hold_fast hp_regen electric), element=fire|ice|explosion|electric, pierce.
