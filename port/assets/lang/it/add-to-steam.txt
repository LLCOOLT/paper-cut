Add to Steam
Aggiungi a Steam

Puts Paper Cut in the Steam library as a non-Steam game, with its own artwork and a controller set-up (the Steam Deck's right pad as a mouse). Steam must be closed; a copy of its list is kept.
Aggiunge Paper Cut alla libreria di Steam come gioco non Steam, con le sue immagini e una configurazione del controller (il trackpad destro dello Steam Deck come mouse). Steam deve essere chiuso; viene conservata una copia del suo elenco.
