# Tayce T.'s recipes: Paper Mario 64's dishes at Zess T.'s (docs/PM64_EXTRAS.md)

Tayce T.'s recipes

With your Paper Mario ROM linked: Zess T. cooks Paper Mario's dishes from its recipes too: Fried Shroom, Hot Shroom, Yummy Meal, Deluxe Feast, Kooky Cookie, Super Soda and more, with their names, descriptions, icons and effects from your ROM. Rowf (Star Haven corner, Rogueport's plaza) sells the Paper Mario ingredients they need. In the Start menu's recipe book. Online only when every player has a ROM linked.
