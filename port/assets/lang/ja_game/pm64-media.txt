Your music: the music folder's files play in place of the tracks they are named after. Shuffled: each room's music is another of the game's, field music for field music, battle music for battle music; scenes, jingles and fanfares stay as they are. With yours, the folder's files join in. Paper Mario 64's: with your Paper Mario ROM linked (Enhancements > Paper Mario 64), each place and battle plays a fitting Paper Mario song, everywhere or in about half the areas. Only you hear it.

Paper Mario 64 enemies
マリオストーリーの　てき

Also in the Enemy maker. Not online.

Paper Mario 64 badges
マリオストーリーの　バッジ

With your Paper Mario ROM linked: Rowf comes from Toad Town to the Star Haven corner of Rogueport's plaza with Paper Mario badges TTYD hasn't got: Dizzy Stomp, Mega Jump, Mega Smash, Power Quake, Mega Quake, Triple Dip, Damage Dodge, Peekaboo, Pay-Off and Attack FX A to E, with their names, descriptions and icons from your ROM. Online only when every player has a ROM linked.

Paper Mario sound effects
マリオストーリーの　こうかおん

With your Paper Mario ROM linked: the Paper Mario partners, the Paper Mario enemies, the Star Spirits and the Star Haven corner make Paper Mario's own sounds, read from your ROM: Goombario's Headbonk, Kooper's shell, Bombette's fuse and blast, Parakarry's wings, Watt's sparks, Sushie's splash, Lakilester's cloud and Spiny. Off: the game's own sounds. Only you hear it.
リンクした　マリオストーリーの　ROMで　マリオストーリーの　なかまと　てき　スターの　せい　スターヘイブンの　いっかくが　ROMから　よんだ　マリオストーリーの　おとを　ならします　クリオの　ずつき　カメキの　こうら　ピンキーの　どうかせんと　ばくはつ　パレッタの　はばたき　アカリンの　でんき　おプクの　みずしぶき　ポコピーの　くもと　トゲゾー　オフで　ゲームの　おと　きこえるのは　あなただけです
