# The partner wheel in Japanese for the JP release's own font (the Start menu's Settings tab): kana and its few kanji, words apart.

Partner wheel
なかま　ホイール

Hold Partner quick-swap ({SWAP}, or the on-screen swap button) for a ring of your partners; point at one with a stick, the movement keys, the mouse or your finger, and let go. A quick tap still swaps to the next one (with Partner quick-swap on).
なかまの　クイックチェンジを　ながおしすると　なかまの　わが　でます　ひとりを　えらんで　はなします
