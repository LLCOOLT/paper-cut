# Music shuffle, your own music and the input display: the Start menu's Settings tab (the JP font's characters only)

Music shuffle
音がく　シャッフル

Each room's music is another of the game's: field music for field music, battle music for battle music. Scenes, jingles and fanfares stay as they are. Only you hear it.
へやの　音がくが　ゲームの　ほかの　きょくに　なります（イベントと　ファンファーレは　そのまま）

Your music
じぶんの　音がく

Your files in the music folder play in place of the game's tracks they are named after.
musicフォルダの　ファイルが　おなじ　名前の　きょくの　かわりに　ながれます

Shuffle includes your music
シャッフルに　じぶんの　音がくも

Music shuffle also picks the music folder's files.
シャッフルが　musicフォルダの　ファイルも　えらびます

Input display
コントローラー　ひょうじ

The controller as the game reads it, in a corner: sticks, buttons, d-pad and triggers (with Second player on, the second controller beside it).
コントローラーの　スティックと　ボタンを　すみに　だします

Music folder
おんがくの　フォルダ
