# Paper looks and the pop-up launcher (port/settings/features/paper_visuals.cpp), in Japanese for the JP release's own font: an English line, then its translation, then a blank line.

Paper shadows
かみの　かげ

Characters cast soft shadows shaped like their paper cut-outs on the floor in front of them, turned by the lamps around them, instead of the round ones. High: longer, darker and sharper, with a dark contact shadow at the feet.
キャラクターが　まるい　かげの　かわりに　かみの　きりぬきの　かたちの　やわらかい　かげを　あしもとの　まえに　おとし　まわりの　ランプで　むきが　かわります　たかい：より　ながく　こく　くっきりした　かげと　あしもとの　こい　かげ

Paper physics
かみの　うごき

Characters bend like paper: they lean into fast moves, flutter on quick turns and squash on hard landings. Only the look changes.
キャラクターが　かみのように　しなります：はやい　うごきで　かたむき　ターンで　ひらひらし　つよい　ちゃくちで　つぶれます（みためだけ）

Smooth paper edges
なめらかな　かみの　ふち

Clean outlines on the paper cut-outs at high resolutions instead of jagged steps; smoothest with anti-aliasing (MSAA) on.
かみの　きりぬきの　ふちが　ギザギザでなく　きれいに　なります（MSAAが　オンで　いちばん　なめらか）

Pop-up launcher
とびだす　えほんの　ランチャー

The launcher's stage builds like a pop-up book as it opens: the curtains part, the title unfolds and Mario and his partners spring up (once the game has run on that disc). Any button skips it.
ランチャーを　ひらくと　ぶたいが　とびだす　えほんのように　くみあがります：まくが　ひらき　マリオと　なかまが　とびだします　どの　ボタンでも　スキップ
