# Level sync, fun toggles, chaos mode, room transitions and battery saver in Japanese for the JP release's own font (the Start menu's Settings tab): kana and its few kanji, words apart.

Level sync
レベルシンク

Enemies below Mario's level are brought up to it: more HP, harder hits, and Star Points as for an even fight. Post-game and New Game+: after the credits, or while Mario is well above the chapter's usual level.
マリオより　レベルの　ひくい　てきが　マリオの　レベルまで　つよく　なります　クリアの　あとと　つよくて　ニューゲームの　ときだけ

Post-game and New Game+
クリアの　あとと　つよくて　ニューゲーム

Always
いつも

Big heads
大きな　あたま

みんなの　あたまが　ほぼ　２ばいの　大きさに　なります

Moon gravity
月の　じゅうりょく

Mario jumps and walks as on the Moon, everywhere.

Paper-thin from every side
どこから　見ても　紙の　うすさ

Chaos mode
カオスモード

Every 30 seconds something happens: giant or tiny Mario, flipped controls, the camera upside down, coin rain, a Bob-omb, and in battle the crowd's tricks. Not online or in replays.
30びょうごとに　なにか　おきる　オンライン　リプレイでは　なし

Room transitions
部屋の　きりかわり

Doors, pipes and room changes turn the old room away like a page, with the game's own page animations, instead of fading to black.
ドアや　どかんで　部屋が　かわる　とき　くらく　なる　かわりに　まえの　部屋を　ページのように　めくります

Battery saver
バッテリーセーバー

Draws 30 frames a second (the game still runs at 60), at half the internal resolution and without in-between frames. Online and in replays every frame is still drawn.
１びょうに　３０まいだけ　えがきます　ゲームは　６０の　まま　うごきます　オンラインと　リプレイでは　ぜんぶ　えがきます
