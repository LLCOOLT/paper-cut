# Colour-blind filters and captions for sounds (port/settings/features/accessibility.cpp) in Japanese for the JP release's own font (the Start menu's Settings tab): kana and its few kanji, words apart. The captions themselves are drawn by the overlay (ja/accessibility.txt).

Colour-blind filter
色の　みえかた　フィルター

Shifts the colours your eyes confuse into ones they can tell apart, over the whole picture (a daltonization correction, not a simulation).
みわけにくい　色を　みわけやすい　色に　ずらします　画面　ぜんたいに　かかります

Protanopia (red-blind)
１がた（赤）

Deuteranopia (green-blind)
２がた（みどり）

Tritanopia (blue-blind)
３がた（青）

Filter strength
フィルターの　つよさ

Captions for sounds
こうかおんの　じまく

Short captions low on the screen for the sounds that tell you something: an enemy noticing you, a hidden block, a Star Piece, a guard, a door or switch out of sight.
だいじな　こうかおんを　画面の　下に　みじかく　ひょうじします
