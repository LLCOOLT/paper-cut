# Rewind and faster battles in the Start menu, in the JP release's font (kana and its few kanji:

Rewind
まきもどし

Hold the Rewind key (Backspace) or button and the game steps back through its last seconds; let go to play on from there. Uses some memory and a little time every few frames. Not in online co-op.
まきもどしキー（Backspace）か　ボタンを　おしている　あいだ　ゲームが　さいごの　すうびょうを　もどります　はなすと　そこから　つづきます　メモリと　すこしの　じかんを　つかいます　オンラインでは　つかえません

まきもどしの　ながさ

Rewind button
まきもどしボタン

A controller button that rewinds while held.

Faster battles
バトルを　はやく

Battles play faster, but never while you act: action commands, Stylish moves, the menus and Star Specials stay at normal speed, and guards and Superguards keep their timing. Not in online co-op.
