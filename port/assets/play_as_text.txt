# tag:    the game's message tag (a '*' at its end: a prefix), or empty for any text
# A space in from is any one space or line break of the text's, and the text's own are kept in to. Longer
# texts are tried first. The game's text is Latin-1 (Japanese: Shift-JIS); this file is UTF-8.
