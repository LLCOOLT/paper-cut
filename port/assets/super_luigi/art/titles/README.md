# The chapter card's coin

The card is the game's own (`rels/sys/src/sys.c`): curtain, letters, spinning coin and subtitle ring, nothing else. Its
coin has four faces in `effect_sys.tpl`; on Super Luigi's cards they are Luigi, Blooey, Jerry and the Marvelous Compass,
made from the player's disc by `art/recipes/luigi_medallion.recipe`. `medallion_drawn/` is Luigi's face's fallback.
