# Hizza, the two-headed snake (Super Luigi, chapter 2 boss)

These are the cards for the `c_sl_hizza` rig. It has 8 texels per game unit,
faces -x (so every head looks left), and stands about 64 units tall when
reared (Luigi is 24). There's one head at the end of the neck (`_a`) and one
at the end of the tail (`_b`).

- **Sources:** each card's `.svg` is generated by
  [`../tools/hizza.py`](../tools/hizza.py), using the style kit in
  [`../tools/papercut.py`](../tools/papercut.py). Edit the script and rerun it:
  `python tools/hizza.py`, then `python tools/hizza_preview.py`.
- **`parts.json`** has every card's size, pivot and role, for the rig.
- **`preview.png`** shows the cards assembled at 1:1, and every expression of
  both heads.

## Look

The colours follow the chapter 2 plan (`docs/super_luigi/ch2.md`, New
assets): an olive-green back (`#9aa940`) with darker diamonds (`#56661c`),
and a cream belly (`#ead690`). The compass piece rides on a leather and brass
**collar** round the middle of the body. Both heads have big bulging eyes on
top, two fangs and a forked tongue.

**Telling the heads apart:**

- **Hiss** (head A, on the neck): loud and greedy. Round open eyes, and a
  big grin swept up the cheek, with a dimple.
- **Zah** (head B, on the tail): quiet and sly. Heavy half-lowered lids for
  narrow eyes, a half-smile with one corner up, a dark spiky crest, and an
  old stitched scar across the cheek and lower jaw. The tail segments under
  it carry a darker spine stripe and darker diamonds.

### Draw order, back to front

1. `coil_back`
2. `tail_b1`, `tail_b2`, `tail_b3`
3. The tail head: `tongue_b`, `mouth_b`, `jaw_b`, `head_b`
4. `neck_a1`, `collar`, `neck_a2`, `neck_a3`
5. The front head: `tongue_a`, `mouth_a`, `jaw_a`, `head_a`
6. `coil_front`

Within a head, the tongue, mouth and jaw sit behind the head card.

### The mouth

`jaw_x` carries a dark mouth fan. When the jaw is shut, the fan stays inside
the head's outline, so the head hides it. Opened up to about **20°**, the fan
fills the gap.

For a wider bite (up to about **45°**), show `mouth_x`. It's pinned to the
head at the hinge, sits behind the jaw, and is shown only while the jaw is
past about 20°. Leave it hidden otherwise, or it shows below a closed jaw.
The preview's bite poses use it at 38°.

| R3: 1-bit alpha | **pass** | Every card has 2 alpha levels and 0 partial texels. |

**Notes on the measurements:**

- `mouth_x` measures no outline and no "base fill", because the whole card is
  dark (`#7a1628`, v 0.48). That's intended: it's an interior.
- `tail_b*` measures 8 to 9 px on the right, because the dark spine stripe
  next to the line is counted as line. The line alone is 5 px.

## Rough edges

- The heads' sheen covers 6.7 to 7.3% of the part, at the bottom of R6b's
  7 to 25% range. Its centre is at y 0.46 to 0.50, lower than the game's
  0.2 to 0.35, because the eyes sit on top of the head.
- The game's textures carry CMPR's 4x4 block noise. These PNGs are clean, and
  the game's own compression would add it.
