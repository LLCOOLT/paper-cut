Paper Cut

Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door, in your browser.

Choose your own disc image of the game (ISO, RVZ, GCZ, CISO, WIA, WBFS, NFS or TGC). It stays on this computer: the page reads it where it is and never uploads or copies it.

Or drop it on this page. Saves and settings stay in this browser. A controller works once a button on it is pressed; on a touch screen, touch it for the buttons.

To install it as an app: Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Saves

Your saves and settings are kept in this browser. Take them out as a .zip to keep or move them; bring in a .zip (this page's, or a desktop Paper Cut's user folder zipped) or GameCube card files (.gci).

This browser can't run the game.

It needs WebGPU: a recent Chrome or Edge (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, Android), or a recent Firefox or Safari with WebGPU turned on.
