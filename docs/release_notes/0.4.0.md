Paper Cut 0.4.0 brings **online co-op**, a **second screen** for dual-screen handhelds, the settings inside the game's own Start menu, and a long list of things to change and turn on: free camera, mirror mode, cosmetics, your own music, texture upscaling, bigger text and more. It plays the Japanese (G8MJ01), US (G8ME01) and European (G8MP01) versions of Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door, from the game's C decompilation, with no emulator.

**What's new**

*Playing together*
- **Online co-op:** the launcher's new Online tab. One player hosts and shares their address, the other joins with it. Both need Paper Cut and their own disc of the same release, on any platform: a PC can play with a Mac, a phone or a Steam Deck. Only the controllers' input crosses the network; both games play the same frames and check every second that they still match. The guest plays on the host's save and settings for the session, keeping their own display, sound and controls, and their own saves untouched.
- **Co-op in the field:** the second player's stick walks the partner anywhere; A hops, B talks or hits, X is the partner's ability, L or R switches partners. Mario calls the partner back with Y. In battle the second player has the partner's moves, action commands and guards, as before.
- **Joining is easy:** with co-op on, A on another controller joins as player 2. "1P" and "2P" show over the characters, a notice says when player 2 joins or leaves, and the Players section (Controls) shows who is who and picks player 2's controller with a press (a keyboard and one controller are enough).

*A second screen*
- On dual-screen handhelds like the AYN Thor (Android): the game's own Start menu, live on the lower screen while you play on the upper one, navigated by touch; using an item or switching partners from it works as in the menu. It appears only on devices with a second built-in screen.

*Settings*
- **In the Start menu:** a Settings tab after the Journal, in the game's own windows and font, with every on/off, choice and number, and All settings for the rest.
- **Restart now** beside the settings that apply from the next start: the game starts again and puts you back where you were.
- **In six languages:** the settings, the launcher and the Start menu's tab in English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano or Japanese (System > Menu language).
- **Saves' tools while playing:** restoring a backup or copying a save to another release without quitting.

*Looks and sound*
- **Texture upscaling:** the game's textures 2× or 4× larger, through Paper Cut's own edge-following filter.
- **Free camera:** the right stick turns the camera a little around Mario and moves it in or out.
- **Mirror mode:** the world flipped left to right, the controls turned to match.
- **Cosmetics:** Mario's cap and overalls, Goombella's hair, Vivian and the HUD's bars in 13 colours or any from a picker, with Luigi, Wario, Waluigi and Fire Mario presets.
- **Music shuffle and your own music:** each room's music another of the game's; or your own OGG, MP3, FLAC or WAV files, named after the track they replace.
- **HDR output** on Windows and Mac displays that have it.
- **Volume:** sliders with mute buttons, and F9, F10 and F3 for volume anywhere.

*Controls*
- **Controllers:** the community mapping list (about 2,300 models), players 1 to 4, battery, a live test, dead zones, remapping for every player, and a set-up for controllers nothing knows. They're named as you know them ("DualSense", "Switch Pro Controller"), and so are their buttons.
- **Button prompts** that match your Xbox, PlayStation, Switch or Steam Deck controller, or your keys.
- **Steam Deck:** the first start sets itself up for the Deck's screen.

*Help and play*
- **Bigger text:** message windows up to 150%.
- **"Where next?":** Y on the Journal tab names where the story goes next, and nothing is shown until you ask.
- **Challenges:** presets and four new rules (one-hit KO, no guarding, harder attack commands, no items in battle).
- **Boss refights:** any boss you've beaten, fought again.
- **Built-in randomizer** (US): a seed and what to shuffle, no server needed, with a spoiler log.
- **Autosave** at room changes (off by default), and a one-time note in the first room on where the settings are.
- **Input display** for streams and runs.

*Elsewhere*
- **Linux:** an AppImage and a Flatpak beside the zip.
- **Save sync through any folder** on Windows and Linux (OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive, Syncthing...), as iCloud does on Apple devices.
- **Character export:** a character's model, animations and textures out of the game, edited in Blender, and back as a mod.
- **The same results on every computer:** the game now uses the GameCube's own maths library everywhere, which online play needs.

**You need your own copy of the game:** a disc image (`.iso`, `.rvz`, `.gcm`...). Add it in the launcher.

**Known issues**
- Online co-op has been played between two games on one computer; across two computers, and through routers, it's new. Over the internet the host needs to forward a port, or both players can join a VPN such as Tailscale or ZeroTier. Minimizing the window pauses that player's game, and the session ends after 15 seconds.
- Online, save states, fast-forward, frame advance, the Practice tab's editors, warps and the Twitch chat are off.
- The second screen hasn't run on a real dual-screen device yet.
- The translations are Paper Cut's own, not yet checked by native speakers.
- In mirror mode, signs and text that are part of the 3D world read backwards.
- Nothing is code-signed. Windows SmartScreen warns on first run (More info > Run anyway). On macOS, the first time it's blocked: try to open it, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway. The iOS app must be signed by a sideloading tool.
- Linux and the Steam Deck haven't been played on yet. The AppImage and the Flatpak are new and not yet tried.
