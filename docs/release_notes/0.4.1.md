Paper Cut 0.4.1 is a fix release for 0.4.0, and **the Android apps now play the game**: a disc picked in the file manager opens, and the game runs, in optimized builds like the computers'. Phones also check for updates from now on. It fixes the crashes players hit late in the story (the Excess Express after chapter 6, the Shadow Queen, Grodus and the ship in the ending), the freeze in long cutscenes when playing as Peach, the online file select dropping the session, and the open world's seeds that couldn't be finished. It also fixes many smaller problems found by running every room and story beat of the game unattended. It plays the Japanese (G8MJ01), US (G8ME01) and European (G8MP01) versions of Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door, from the game's C decompilation, with no emulator.

Saves, settings and replays from 0.4.0 carry over. Online, both players need 0.4.1: a 0.4.0 game and a 0.4.1 game can't play together.

**Highlights**

- No more late-story crashes: train, Shadow Queen, ending.
- Long scenes no longer stop; Peach no longer freezes.
- Online, the file select no longer drops the session.
- MSAA no longer crashes after Professor Frankly.
- Android: discs picked in the file manager open.
- Every open-world seed can be finished.
- B+X+Start goes back to the title instead of freezing.
- German and French text that shows; menus that fit.

**What's new**

- **Phones check for updates too**, from 0.4.1 on. Android downloads the new APK, checks it against the release's checksum and hands it to Android's installer, which asks first. On iOS, add Paper Cut's source to AltStore or SideStore once (the notice's Add to AltStore or Add to SideStore, or `https://github.com/LLCOOLT/paper-cut/releases/latest/download/altstore-source.json`), and they offer each new version.

**Fixed**

*Crashes and freezes*
- **The Excess Express after chapter 6:** boarding at Rogueport crashed, and so did later rides through the train's cabins. (#2)
- **Long cutscenes:** a scene that waited more than 53 seconds stopped for good, and playing as Peach could freeze. Waits are measured in 32 bits again, as on the GameCube.
- **The Shadow Queen's second phase** crashed as it started. (#2)
- **Grodus:** crowded battle scenes ran out of room for their poses, and his fourth satellite overwrote his current target. (#2)
- **The ending:** the ship leaving Rogueport crashed. (#2)
- **The 1000 Dry Bones room** could crash after the swarm had pushed Mario about.
- **More message windows:** a scene with more than seven open at once stopped closing them.
- **When the game's memory is full**, Paper Cut now gives it more instead of crashing.
- **Lookups whose target is gone:** a missing event, item, partner, NPC or battle unit no longer crashes the game.
- **A partner's blend effect** with no partner out crashed.
- **Versus:** Gale Force could blow Mario or Luigi out of the fight and crash it.
- **Play as:** a move the character's model doesn't have now plays one it does, instead of freezing.
- **B+X+Start**, the soft reset, goes back to the title instead of freezing. It's refused online.
- **No crash on a position or speed that isn't a number:** an angle to such a point, or a Puni slowing to a stop.

*Online*
- **The file select:** the mouse in the game's menus now goes with the controller, so a click there no longer drops the session.
- **Starting an online game**, or Restart now, keeps the window until the new one has drawn.

*The open world*
- **Every seed can be finished.** The ship to Keelhaul Key, the first train ride and the moon cannon have no way back until their chapter's scene ends, and the logic now knows it. About nine seeds in ten could leave Mario stuck before. The attendant, Flavio and the cannon also wait until Mario can finish the trip. **Open-world seeds give other placements than in 0.4.0**, and a file started in 0.4.0 keeps its own.
- **The Creepy Steeple's letter** no longer skips chapter 4.
- **The sewers' Pit entrance room** no longer crashes, and the Dark Bones room's Palace Key lands on its item again.
- **A partner joining in another's place** shows its joining text.

*Saves and settings*
- **0.3.0 can read 0.4.x's settings.txt** again, and saves from 0.1.0, 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 load as before.
- **Quick resume** keeps no save made inside a house or a shop, where the room doesn't load back whole. Continue from such a save made by 0.4.0 walks Mario in by the map's entrance instead of dropping him through the floor.
- **Android:** a disc picked in the file manager opens, instead of showing as a missing file, and the list shows its real name. Mods picked there are recognised too. (#6)
- **A save restored from the library** takes its records with it.
- **Save states and rewind** put back the screen's timing, the music's place and what was being drawn.

*Graphics*
- **MSAA no longer crashes after Professor Frankly's talk:** with anti-aliasing (MSAA) on, the fence peeling away as Mario leaves his house stopped the game on Windows (D3D12) and on Linux and the Steam Machine (Vulkan). Its shadow is now made from the anti-aliased picture too. (#7, #8)
- **Characters never wear another model's textures:** a Toad on Rogueport's harbor could show Flurrie's face. Textures were read by the drawing thread after the game had already reused their memory; drawing now finishes before memory is freed or moved, and textures and screen captures can no longer outlive their data. (#5)
- **Back at the title after a Game Over or a soft reset,** the file select no longer reads the unloaded map's text.
- **The Tattle Log** no longer freezes or closes the game when an enemy's card opens from the Journal. (#11)
- **Android** no longer stops on the first frame once a disc opens: the APKs are now optimized release builds, like the computers' packages, and run faster too.

*Text and menus*
- **German text:** 11 texts showed in English on the US and EU discs, because their low opening quotes aren't in the game's font. They use the font's own quotes now, and French œ is written oe for the same reason.
- **Long labels shortened** in German, Spanish, French and Italian.
- **On the Japanese disc**, Paper Cut's English text drops its accents (the font has none) instead of falling back.
- **The Save Block's storage menu** speaks the menu's language. It was always English.
- **The opening's narration** sits inside its caption paper again with Message size above 100% (the Steam Deck's default); the ending credits too. (#10)
- **The Start menu's help** breaks its lines by the letters' real width, so translations stay inside the box. Long setting names shrink evenly instead of being squashed sideways.
- **The Star Points after a fight** read their own language's text.
- **Crystal Star scenes** no longer run past the end of their script (US, EU), and the Japanese Start menu no longer reads past its slots on Paper Cut's tabs.
- **Coloured words** in battle and item messages show their colours again.
- **Discord status:** the dot between the details shows as a dot, not "Â·".

*Battles and the world*
- **Versus:** Shell Shield guards only its own side's Mario. The computer holds the stick the right way for Fire Drive and no longer Tattles, which left its window waiting forever.
- **Screen blurs** last their full time again.
- **Co-op companions** keep their own form in the 8-bit pipe.
- **The audience's sound position and some map colours** match the GameCube's for crowds all on one side and keyed colours that overshoot.
- **Smaller fixes, matching the GameCube:** a partner's hop from standing still, the field map coming back after a fight, a battle item's sparkles, a shop's sold-out check, Dark Bones' path, Bowser's footing, very short vectors and Bingo cards past the third.

**You need your own copy of the game:** a disc image (`.iso`, `.rvz`, `.gcm`...) made from your own disc. Paper Cut includes no game data. Add it in the launcher.

**Known issues**
- The known issues of 0.4.0 still apply, apart from those fixed above.
- Most of these fixes were found and checked by scripted runs of the game. The menu and text changes haven't all been looked at by hand in every language yet.
