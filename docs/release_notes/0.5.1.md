On a phone, pick the file for your disc's release (US, JP or EU). On a computer, one download plays all three.

**Updating from 0.5.0:** your saves, settings and replays carry over. To play online, everyone needs 0.5.1.

**Highlights**

- Online holds together: drops heal, rooms come back.
- Paper Mario 64's Spin Dash, with its spin badges.
- Run from Paper Mario 64's bosses.
- Visit Mario's house again from the Journal's map.
- A quick wheel of eight common paper notes.
- Wardrobe colours cover every part, edge to edge.
- Smoother at 120 Hz and above.
- Low on video memory? Graphics step down instead.
- Paper Mario 64 enemies act out every battle state.
- Fixes and features for 15 reported issues.

**What's new**

*Online*
- **Rooms hold together:** games that drift apart find it and heal it. That covers the room's enemies and items, the story and the battle stage.
- **Coming back is quick:** a dropped connection reconnects at once, in the same seat. So does a network change or a phone sent to the background.
- **A host's crash no longer ends the room.** The host can open it again for 15 minutes, with the same code, and the guests wait.
- **Joining shows each step** and says which one is stuck. Keep waiting, Try again or Copy diagnostics from there.
- **A connection icon** in the field and in battle, and Reconnecting... while the link is down (Multiplayer settings).
- **A guest's second controller** presses Start and takes a free seat.
- **Leave a quiz or card duel** as a guest with Z; players 3 and 4 watch the duel.
- **An idle dungeon master** hands the enemies to the game after 15 seconds; any press takes them back.
- **Direct connection:** two games can play straight to each other. The online service stays there to fall back on (Multiplayer, off by default).
- **Each their own game:** a randomizer, Archipelago or hardcore file never mixes with another kind.
- **Room codes much harder to guess,** and the online service keeps out squatters and floods.

*Play*
- **Paper Mario's Spin Dash** in the field, with your Paper Mario ROM linked. Turn it on in the Paper Mario 64 page, then tap L, or press Z on the keyboard. Rowf sells Speedy Spin, Spin Attack and Dizzy Attack. (#51)
- **Special places** on the Journal's map: Mario's house, with your character and partner; the yard's pipe leads back. It opens once you've seen it, with fast travel on, and the story stays as it was. Bowser's Castle and his stages come in a later version. (#48)
- **The remake's pipe room** in the sewers, a new remake switch. Pipes to Rogueport's plaza, Glitzville, Twilight Town and Fahr Outpost appear once their Crystal Stars are in.
- **A second barrel** in the Pirate's Grotto's floodgate room after chapter 5, as in the remake.
- **Spring Jump from standing only:** no Spring Jump straight out of a landing. In Enhancements, off by default. (#53, #55, #57, #64)
- **Take back your own paper note** from its balloon, or from the Start menu's notes page. Your notes stay yours on this device, even if your internet address changes.
- **Paper notes are on by default** again (Multiplayer > Paper notes).
- **A notes quick wheel:** hold the notes button where no note is near. Pick one of eight common notes.

*Controllers and touch*
- **Everything the keyboard does, a controller does too,** with Back held. Back + Start goes to the launcher, Back + Y takes a screenshot, Back + D-pad saves and loads a state, Back + B rewinds. Change them in Controls > Bindings.
- **An on-screen keyboard** for room codes, names and search when you type with a controller.
- **Switch Pro Controller and Joy-Cons:** A, B, X and Y are the buttons printed A, B, X and Y. (#60)
- **Rate paper notes with a tap** on phones and tablets.
- **The second screen's menu** does everything the Start menu does, on dual-screen handhelds. It also comes back after its screen turns or changes.

*Settings*
- **Every setting says plainly what it does.** 72 settings got clearer names and descriptions, in every language.
- **Settings take effect in the room you're in.** The remake's pipes and blocks, merchants, Whacka and more appear or go at once.
- **Restore everything:** one switch at the top of Restorations, as the remake page has.
- **Back to the launcher** on the Start menu's Settings front page (it asks first).
- **Four extras start off:** Team bonus, Duo moves, idle players in the audience and stream chat's harmful commands. A choice you made is kept.

*Play as*
- **Peach's pronouns:** where the text means Mario, it says she and her. In every language.
- **Titles and nicknames follow your pick;** the mustache nicknames become Peach's crown or Bowser's horns, and Luigi is called little brother.
- **Bowser is never seen from behind:** walking away, he uses his front walk.
- **Peach's parasol float** keeps level with Mario's same jump. A running jump no longer falls short, and the parasol opens everywhere.
- **Wardrobe colours cover every part, edge to edge,** with no spots of the old colour or tinted edges.

*Paper Mario 64*
- **Its enemies act out every battle state.** A Koopa Troopa falls on its back, and asleep, dizzy, frozen or burnt each have their own look.
- **Cool Mode's glasses** stay on the faces of Paper Mario 64's partners, enemies and bosses.
- **Each Paper Mario 64 boss** has a Tattle of its own.
- **Run Away works against Paper Mario 64's bosses** in Rogueport and their refights. Boss Rush still allows no running.

*Graphics*
- **Widescreen effects reach the edges.** Wipes, curtains, the star flash and Art Attack fill the screen, and battles no longer show black past the stage.
- **Smoother at 120 Hz and up,** with no judder when the frame rate is above the display's.
- **Running out of video memory** steps the graphics down instead of closing the game. (#56)
- **Direct3D 11 on Windows** (Graphics API), for older or Intel graphics drivers.
- **Safe graphics** asks the least of the graphics card. It's in Graphics, and offered after the graphics stop the game.
- **Android: OpenGL ES** as a Graphics API choice. The system's game modes no longer cap the frame rate at 30.
- **Phones guard their memory.** They step the graphics down instead of being closed, and say when the system closed the game.

*And more*
- **Crash-safe snapshots:** after a crash, continue from a few minutes before. Or load your save as usual (General, on by default).
- **Undo a setting that crashed the game:** the next start asks.
- **Report a problem:** one zip with what's needed, shown before it's made. In Settings > General and the launcher's About page.
- **Spoiler logs and mods easy to find.** The Randomizer page opens the spoiler logs folder, and the Mods tab shows the mods folder. (#62)
- **The Paper Cut Discord** on the launcher's About page.

**Fixed**

- **Crashes:** cooking two ingredients at Zess T.'s, a Paper Mario 64 enemy's first battle, Art Attack above 60 fps or in stereo 3D, Archipelago right after the opening, Rogueport's plaza after the story with 4x textures, and an online join refused at once. Also a likely cause of crashes in online battles in Boggly Woods with Punio following (#59).
- **The game opens** when the graphics driver fails to start Direct3D 12, and on Windows with Bottom-up or High-entropy ASLR off.
- **Adreno handhelds:** fixes for GPU resets, such as the AYN Thor's at Rogueport's harbour (#39). Lighter graphics on phones too.
- **The Spring Jump:** A jumps again instead of a Spring Jump after every landing with the Ultra Boots. (#53, #55, #57, #64)
- **Fast-forward on the Steam Deck:** Mario and the partners speed up too. The same on any screen that holds frames back. (#63)
- **Vivian's tail** no longer lingers on screen when she sinks into her shadow. In battle, the field and Paper Mode. (#54)
- **Remake pipes** stand at the rooms' own height, chapter 4's too. Twilight Town's opens only from the Trail's end. (#49)
- **"!" prompts** show only over what's there: no more "!" at random spots with the remake rules on.
- **Open world:** the way into Boggly Woods opens, and chapter 8 no longer needs chapter 7 done. (#65)
- **Riding Yoshi into a scene** no longer leaves Mario stuck in the saddle (open world).
- **Fast travel and the warp map** reach every room, the Shhwonk Fortress's too. (#61)
- **Tutorials:** X and Y are the buttons that work, and X stops an audience throw again. (#60)
- **Online:** friends who join from their Start menu no longer see a black screen; Punio stays after a partner swap; players 3 and 4 get their character at once when they join late.
- **Brothers mode online:** Luigi and the partners walk, stop and face on everyone's screen as on their player's, instead of walking in place or facing away. A talk's text boxes and choices stay on every screen, page by page, until they close. A guest's Luigi stops at the floor's edge by a room's exit instead of walking off and falling.
- **The Flatpak installs again,** and Add to Steam starts it.
- **Archipelago** connects on Linux and macOS, and says when the TTYD AP mod is missing.
- **Holding Start and Back** opens the settings page again on a controller with no Settings button.
- **Text:** Japanese button names in the Start menu, and Italian lines when you play as Peach.
- **Graphics are prepared up front:** no stutter the first time something new appears, such as paper notes and talks. On Android too.
- **Texture upscaling** no longer makes scenes stutter when many new textures appear (cutscenes, Paper Mario 64 sprites).
- **Paper Mario 64 sprites move as in Paper Mario:** Bow bobs again, and so do 40 more whose parts follow a later part.
- **Brothers mode:** Luigi finds his own place in scenes, clear of the partner and NPCs, no longer hops over and over in cutscenes, and is with Mario after every door, building, room change and lift. In houses you walk into, such as Rogueport's inn, he's lit like Mario instead of dark. He copies each of Mario's jumps once, never lands on top of him, and goes up a ledge with him.
- **Brothers mode's Luigi heals too:** Heart Blocks, inns, Chet Rippo, items and level-ups fill his HP and FP, and the audience count no longer covers his HP in battle. Quick resume keeps his HP and FP as they were.
- **Autosave reaches the Pit of 100 Trials,** floor after floor; a save there loads on that floor. The Paper Tower and the co-op Pit keep their deepest floor, and the endless Glitz Pit its most rounds, as you go. Autosave stays off by default (General). (#71)
- **Battle puzzles:** after a puzzle that lends a partner, your file's own partner is out again, not the lent one.
- **A mode's line at the top** (the Pit's round, the boss rush's clock, the tower's floor, the raid's bar) hides while a battle's speech bubble is up.
- **Paper Mario 64 enemies** follow the difficulty's enemy HP and level sync, like the game's own.
- **Mario's Paper Mario 64 look** is his usual height and swings Paper Mario 64's own hammer, frame for frame. Paper Mario 64 sprites no longer turn dark when seen edge-on, and their enemies' turned parts sit as in Paper Mario.
- **The Spin Dash** moves, turns and poses as in Paper Mario 64, with its badges' coloured ribbons.
- **The Spin Dash** ends straight into a walk or run while you hold the stick, and spinning into an exit or off an edge no longer drops Mario off the map.
- **On harder difficulties,** bosses change phase and the Fuzzy Horde flees at the same share of their HP as on Normal.
- **Online co-op:** a partner following Mario no longer gets stuck hopping far behind him.
- **Versus:** leaving a fight from its command menu ends it at once.
- **Achievements won with a badge** show its picture.
- **Wardrobe colours** stay inside their lists: Random, Reset and looks never pick a colour that isn't there.

**Known issues**
- The GPU fixes for the AYN Thor (#39) haven't been tried on a Thor yet. A log from the device would help.
- A crash at chapter 6's last pipe (#68) hasn't been reproduced yet. A save and the crash report would help.
- 120 Hz has been tested on a 60 Hz display only; if it judders on a 120 Hz or faster display, turn on Variable refresh rate in Display settings.

**You need your own copy of the game:** a disc image (`.iso`, `.rvz`, `.gcm`...) made from your own disc. Paper Cut includes no game data. For the Paper Mario 64 features, your own Paper Mario ROM too.
