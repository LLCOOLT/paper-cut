**Updating from 0.4.1:** your saves, settings and replays carry over. The widescreen HUD is turned on once; Display > Widescreen HUD at the edges turns it off again. To play online, everyone needs 0.5.0.

**Highlights**

- Co-op reworked: Start joins, a card for every player.
- A steadier online connection; phones join computers.
- Paper Mario 64's partners and bosses, from your ROM.
- The Switch remake's changes, one switch for all.
- The randomizer on every version, with entrance shuffle.
- A map editor: build rooms, share them as mods.
- Restorations of the Japanese release.
- Bowser breathes fire, Peach floats, Luigi jumps higher.
- Skip any scene, fast travel and Jump to chapter.
- Presets, and 115 new achievements.
- Fixes for 27 reported issues, and many more.

**What's new**

*Playing together*
- **One Multiplayer page,** on the title menu and in the Start menu: Brothers mode (player 2 plays Luigi), Online, Party games and Who's who.
- **Press Start to join.** Player 1 is always the first controller; each new player takes the next seat, with a note on screen.
- **Your own card:** pick your character, swap seats, emote or check your buttons, without pausing anyone.
- **Emotes:** a wheel of the game's own speech bubbles. Hold Z.
- **The camera fits everyone** in the picture.
- **In the audience:** a player with no one in a battle sits in the crowd and throws the odd Mushroom, or a rock at an enemy.
- **Team bonus:** when everyone lands their action command in a turn, a little extra Star Power.
- **Party games online** with players 3 and 4, and versus online played to the end.

*Online* (#16)
- **The host's world leads** and everyone else follows it, so a slow or lossy connection keeps playing, and a player who drops comes back and catches up.
- **Any mix of devices:** Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iPhone in one room, with the same disc release.
- **Guests keep a copy** of the adventure at the end of a session, and earn achievements too.

*Paper Mario 64, from your own ROM* (#9)
- **Link your ROM** (`.z64`, `.v64`, `.n64`) in the launcher or the Start menu. Nothing from the N64 game ships with Paper Cut: it's read from your ROM, on your device.
- **Eight partners:** Goombario, Kooper, Bombette, Parakarry, Bow, Watt, Sushie and Lakilester, with their own field abilities and their own moves in battle, rebuilt from Paper Mario's own.
- **Win them from their bosses,** who wait in Rogueport as the story goes on.
- **Ten bosses** from Jr. Troopa to Bowser, with their own music and effects. Refight them any time, scaled to your level.
- **More, as you like:** its enemies in the story, Rowf's badges, Tayce T.'s recipes, the Star Spirits, Mario's N64 look and the N64 soundtrack. Or **Everything Paper Mario 64** with one switch.

*The Switch remake's changes, as options*
- **One switch, Play it like the Switch remake,** or pick from the Remake page one by one. All off by default.
- **Battles:** the remake's balance and rules.
- **Maps:** new Save and Heart Blocks, the sewers' warp pipe, shortcut pipes, merchants and more.
- **The field:** the hammer's backswing, Koops' aim, a steadier Paper Plane, 9,999 coins, bigger bags and repeated-scene skips.

*Play as, further*
- **Bowser breathes fire** in the field and in battle, and spits fireballs. (#24)
- **Peach floats** under her parasol and throws it.
- **Luigi jumps** a little higher and floatier.
- **Their own voices** wherever Mario's plays.

*Getting started*
- **Thirteen presets,** from Vanilla to Story, Challenge, Couch party, Accessible, Paper Mario 64, Randomizer and Speedrun. Your first start asks how you want to play.
- **Skip any scene:** hold Start (on by default); B stops a skip. **Fly through text:** hold B.
- **Skip Peach's and Bowser's interludes,** after you've seen them or always.
- **Start in Rogueport** skips the opening.
- **What's new** after each update, tips, and a multiplayer help page.

*Menus*
- **Back out of anything** with B, Esc, a right click, the touch controls' B or Android's back button.
- **B is B in every menu,** whatever your controller.
- **F2** goes back to the launcher, and asks first.
- **Anything that can't happen right now says why.**

*Graphics*
- **Ray-traced lighting** (Windows and Linux) and **paper shadows** you can see.
- **The widescreen HUD** sits at the screen's edges (on by default).
- **Internal resolution up to 4K and beyond,** now named by its lines (480p to 3840p).
- **Smoother at 120 Hz and up,** and a game that keeps its speed when the graphics card falls behind.
- **Older PCs** make new graphics in the background instead of freezing.
- **Cool Mode:** sunglasses on everyone.

*And more*
- **Restorations** of seven things the Japanese release had, each its own switch: Rogueport's chalk outline, the Parlor Boo's bunny ears, 1000 X-Nauts and more.
- **After the story:** Whacka in the Pit, the Pit Mover to floor 50, two superbosses, the Paper Tower, a trophy room and more.
- **The randomizer on every version,** with entrance shuffle and a tracker in the Journal.
- **The map editor:** place the game's own pipes, blocks and NPCs, and save it as a mod.
- **Fast travel** from the Journal's map, and **Jump to chapter** in Practice. (#3, #4, #13)
- **Linux** from Ubuntu 22.04 (#21), **Linux handhelds** through PortMaster, and **portable mode** (#19).
- **Touch controls sized for your screen** on phones and tablets. (#28)
- **A second screen** on dual-screen Android handhelds such as the AYN Thor.
- **A save manager** for Dolphin, GameShark and Action Replay saves.
- **181 achievements** in nine groups.

**Fixed**

- **Crashes:** quitting through Steam or logging out was reported as a crash (#27); restarting on SteamOS could crash (#17, #20, #25); picking up an upgrade the randomizer moved (#32, #36); the Change Partner list's HP (#42); a Save Block's empty window (#40); supersampling running out of memory (#40); time zones on Windows (#33); and crashes with Swoopulas, Chain Chomps, shops, the Glitz Pit roster, Rowf, Brothers mode and some partner moves.
- **Graphics:** half the screen white on some Android tablets and AMD graphics under Linux (#30); missing maps on older Adreno graphics (#30); Bowser's back view (#12); Mario in bed (#22); the Bowser stages' digits (#43); and bloom in widescreen.
- **Text and menus:** Goombella's Tattle of Paper Cut's own characters (#23), Bow's name, long text, four-digit coins, and mode boxes and lists that overlapped.
- **Modes:** Mid and Late Game loadouts give their boots and hammer again (#34), and boss fights play their music.
- **Co-op:** a controller no longer becomes player 2 by itself, and a battle never waits on one player.
- **Duo and Bros. moves** no longer darken the second character.
- **Picking a file** on iPhone and Android works the first time.

*Already fixed in 0.4.1:* the crash with anti-aliasing under Vulkan on Linux (#26, #35).

**Known issues**
- Online, a guest's second controller can't take a seat yet.
- Paper Mario 64's bosses wait in Rogueport, not yet in the chapters themselves.
- On the AYN Thor, the slowdown at Rogueport's harbour (#39) isn't solved yet. A log from the device would help.
- The Linux handheld build and the dual-screen Android menu haven't been played on a real device yet.

**You need your own copy of the game:** a disc image (`.iso`, `.rvz`, `.gcm`...) made from your own disc. Paper Cut includes no game data. For the Paper Mario 64 features, your own Paper Mario ROM too.
