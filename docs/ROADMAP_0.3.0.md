# Paper Cut 0.3.0: where it stands

All the work is on the branch `feature/0.3.0` (from `main` at 0.2.0). Super Luigi is on its own branch, untouched, and comes in a later release.

**Everything agreed for 0.3.0 is done. JP, US and EU build** on Windows (2026-11-03), with no errors, and so does the headless build. Each feature below was tried once in the US game on Windows, through the test driver (`$TTYD_INPUT`), the settings page's test switches and window captures. Nothing has been tried on a phone, a Mac or Linux yet, and `tools/port/local_ci.py` hasn't been run on the branch.

**Polish after the first tries (2026-11-04):** the sections' own text (buttons, lists, status lines, the launcher's) in all five languages; the explanations that said "below" or "this page" reworded to name the setting, as the Start menu's tab shows them too; the co-op notices' longer translations break onto two lines (or shrink) to fit the picture.

**Fixed while trying them:**
- Autosave never saved: it forgot the room whenever the game left the field, and every room change goes through the map-change sequence.
- The Saves tab drew its two sections' headings twice (also in 0.2.0).
- A settings-page crash the Settings tab's first version had: a section's draw function read as a setting.
- The iCloud Drive section was missing from the Mac's and iPhone's settings page (also in 0.2.0): the settings library wasn't built with `PORT_CLOUD_SYNC`.

**Known gaps:**
- The randomizer's key-item mode needs the TTYD AP mod (built separately, GPL).
- HDR10/PQ isn't done.
- The Settings tab's label sits on a plain shape, not painted like the game's.
- The translations are Claude's, with the games' own names for their terms where earlier ones read them from the discs' text; a native speaker hasn't checked them. The 0.3.0 additions (the sections' own text, about 290 per language) use the names the earlier translations have; the Trouble Center's and the Pianta Parlor's are described rather than named. Brand names (Xbox, PlayStation) and button letters stay as printed.
- The Linux packages haven't been built: the AppImage needs appimagetool (in the container now), and the Flatpak, written without `flatpak-builder`, takes its runtime (org.freedesktop.Platform 24.08) from Flathub on install.
- Texture upscaling skips textures larger than 512 texels a side and those uploaded as they are (block-compressed ones); at 4x a big room's textures take 16 times the video memory.

## Left out of 0.3.0

- **The mod browser** (the user, 2026-11-03: left out until there are mods to list). It downloaded a catalogue of mods (`mods/catalogue.json` on `main`) and installed, updated and uninstalled them from the Mods tab, with each zip checked against the catalogue's sha256 and refused if it could write outside the mods folder. Only our three example mods were in it. It is in this branch's history: commit `646ab562b` (and its `docs/MODDING.md` section); `port/settings/http.cpp`, which it brought, stays for the update check.
- **An open-world randomizer of our own** (the user, 2026-11-03: the next version's, not 0.3.0's). Key items shuffled without the TTYD AP mod: the built-in randomizer's logic already has the apworld's 403 locations and rules (MIT); what's missing is the game opened up, an "open world" setting that sets the story flags and opens Rogueport's pipes and the doors the rules assume open, and each area's map scripts changed where the story assumes an item or a finished chapter (27 areas; the AP mod's changes are about 18,500 lines). It is to be written from the decomp and the apworld alone: the TTYD AP mod is GPL, so its code is not a reference. Suggested in stages (the core, Rogueport and chapters 1-2; then 3-5; then 6-8 and the Palace), each checked with scripted runs that hand out the key items in an unusual order, and whole seeds played through at the end.
