# Playing together

Mario's player and up to three more, on one computer, online, or both at once.

## The Multiplayer page

One page for all of it: the title menu's **Multiplayer** button, or the Start
menu's **Multiplayer** tab. Online play lives only here. The adventure needs no
button: co-op is on by default (Multiplayer settings, Second player), so a
controller's **Start** joins at any time, and the page's header says so: the
open seats, and **Start** on a controller (its own Start icon, even while you
play on the keyboard).

- **Brothers mode:** player 2 plays Luigi beside Mario ([BROTHERS.md](BROTHERS.md)).
- **Online...:** host a room and send its code, join one by code, from the room
  list or by address, an invite link's room, watching, and the room's options
  ([STATE_SYNC.md](STATE_SYNC.md)). **Host a room** opens the room at once,
  in a game or over the title. From the title, with no file, the host then
  picks **Party...** (no save needed) or closes the page and picks **Play**:
  once the host is in a file, everyone comes into that adventure. Until the
  host picks, guests wait in the room.
- **Party...:** a picture menu of seven party games: Versus, Raid boss, Boss
  rush, Quiz party, Tattle-card duel, Bingo and Dungeon master (pick who
  commands the bosses). The host picks; everyone goes straight in, no save
  needed ([MODES.md](MODES.md#party-the-multiplayer-page)).
- **Who's who...:** each seat and who sits there (a controller's name, or an
  online player's).
- **Close / Back:** a button under the page, on every page and list of it:
  tap or click it, or press **B** (Esc on the keyboard). Close leaves the menu
  (over the title, back to its buttons); Back goes back to the page's front.
- **Team bonus:** with two players or more, when everyone who acts in a turn
  lands their action command (Nice or better), a "Team bonus!" pop-up and a
  quarter of a Star Power bar. On by default (also Multiplayer > Team bonus);
  online it's the host's setting and the host's world gives it.

**From the launcher:** plain **Play** (or **New adventure** when you have no
save yet); the launcher has no rooms or codes of its own. To play online, the
host picks **Multiplayer**, **Online...**, **Host a room**, in a game or from
the title. Once the host is in a file, the room plays the host's adventure. Friends use **Online...**, **Join by code** and the code;
they never pick a file: their game goes straight into the host's adventure, in
the host's room, in their seat. A friend joining later lands in the host's
room the same way.

**Invite links** (`papercut://join/<code>`): opened before the game, the
launcher starts the chosen disc on the Multiplayer page, where **Join the
invite's room** waits at the top; it never joins by itself. With no disc yet,
the Play tab says so and the game opens there once one is added.

**Joining:** press **Start** on any controller that has no seat, at any time
(the title, the Multiplayer page, the field; in a battle it joins at the battle's
end): it takes the next free seat, and a note at that player's place says so
("2P joined", with Start for their own menu). Only Start joins: a controller
never becomes another player by itself. The first controller, a handheld's
built-in controls (an AYN Odin, a Steam Deck) or the first one paired with a
phone, is always player 1, and the touch controls hide while it plays; a
second controller connected or pressed is nobody until its Start. That holds
after a restart too: when nobody's controller is player 1's, the first one that
isn't a player becomes player 1's, wherever the last session left it. A seat
kept for a controller that went away is that controller's: another one plugged
in meanwhile plays only once its own Start joins. In a versus fight, Luigi's
side is read from a second controller only once it is a player (joined, or its
A on the versus set-up page); otherwise both sides take turns on the first
controller. Friends who
join online take the next seats the same way, so two players on one screen and
a third online play together. The online part only adds the room code.

## Who's who

The seats: **1** Mario, **2** the partner (Luigi with Brothers mode on), **3**
a companion (the story's follower when there is one), **4** a second
companion. With Brothers mode on, player 4 gets a companion too.

- **On one computer:** A on one seat, then A on another swaps them: the two
  controllers trade places.
- **Online:** A on a seat asks for it; its player sees **Swap seats: yes /
  no** at the top of the page and says yes with one button. The host can move
  anyone directly (A on a player's seat, then A on the seat to put them on).
  Online a guest can play Mario too: their controller plays Mario in the
  host's game, and the host takes the seat they gave up. Mario's player has
  the Start menu (online, their own game's), and the host's game still saves.
- A swap waits for the next calm moment: in the field, with no scene or battle
  running. The page says so meanwhile.

The table below is what each seat plays:

| Player | Plays | In the field | In battle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mario | as always | Mario's turns and guards |
| 2 | the partner | its stick walks the partner (Multiplayer → **Co-op (up to 4 players)**); before Mario has one, a lent partner | the partner's turns and guards |
| 3 | the follower | the story's follower when there is one (Flavio, Frankly...), else a **companion** | the partner's, when it's their companion's partner |
| 4 | a companion | a companion of their own | likewise |

A **companion** is another partner, in the form the game uses for a partner
tagging along (Ms. Mowz first, then Yoshi, Vivian, Flurrie, Bobbery, Koops,
Goombella: those Mario has, never one already on the field or the other
player's). It comes out at Mario's side once a room has settled; **L** or
**R** gives its player the next one. Each walks, hops (A), talks to an NPC or
hits a block or switch in reach (B), and follows Mario while its player
leaves the stick alone, as player 2's partner does. Mario's player calls them
all back with Y. "3P" (green) and "4P" (yellow) show over them, beside "1P"
and "2P".

**The camera and how far they go.** With Multiplayer → **Camera** on **Fit
everyone** (the default), the field camera pans toward the group and pulls
back a little (at most a quarter further) so everyone stays in the picture,
easing in and out. It stays inside each room's own camera limits (the room's
camera road still places it, and it pulls back less where the road holds it
at the room's end); scenes, scripted cameras and battles keep the game's own
camera. A player who walks to the picture's edge is held there, pushing
against it; one the picture leaves behind (Mario walked on) walks back toward
Mario by itself, its stick aside until it's back in the picture. A character
is put back at Mario's side only when it falls (a pit, deep water, well below
Mario's floor, seconds in the air), gets stuck walking back, when a scene
starts (a talk or a menu leaves it where it is) or the room changes. **Follow
Mario** keeps the camera on Mario as in one player's game; the edge holds the
same way.

Every player always has someone. When none of Mario's partners is free (early
in the game, before he has met them), players 3 and 4 get a **lent partner**:
one he hasn't met yet, in the order the story brings them (Goombella, Koops,
Flurrie, Yoshi, Vivian, Bobbery, then Ms. Mowz), never the same for two
players. Before Mario has any partner at all (the prologue, before Goombella
joins), player 2 gets one too (not in Brothers mode, where player 2 is
Luigi): so with four players at a new file's start, player 2 walks a lent
Goombella, player 3 Koops and player 4 Flurrie. A lent partner only tags
along: it walks, hops, talks and hits blocks like any companion, but has no
field ability (player 2's X does nothing with one) and no turns in battle,
and it isn't in the party: no menu, Party tab, partner swap or save ever
shows it, and it gives nothing. **L** or **R** gives its player the next lent
one. A lent partner (and a companion that is the same partner as another
character out) is drawn tinted in its player's colour: player 2's blue,
player 3's green, player 4's yellow, a light tint of the model's own colours,
so it reads as itself and isn't mistaken for the real one. It may stand
beside the story's own (a lent Koops before Koops joins); the colour tells
them apart. When Mario's first partner comes out, player 2's lent one goes and player
2 plays that partner, as always; the others' lent partners move along so each
still has someone. When a lent partner really joins it is simply a companion
Mario has (or, if it comes out as his partner, its player gets the next one),
and once one of his partners is free again a lent one makes way for it.

Enemies notice every player's character, not only Mario: the partner while
player 2 walks it, players 3 and 4's companions and lent partners, and
Luigi in Brothers mode. An enemy chases whichever of them is nearest, and
one that touches any of them (or is walked into) starts the battle. Such a
touch is a plain contact: nobody gets a first strike, neither the players
nor the enemy, and Bump Attack doesn't end it. First strikes stay Mario's
and Luigi's own attacks (a jump or a hammer). With one player nothing
changes. Online, each enemy is the room owner's game's, as Mario's touch is.

In battle the partner fighting belongs to whoever has it in the field: switch
to Yoshi (the battle menu's partner change) and Yoshi's player takes his
turns and guards. A player with no character in the fight watches from the
audience (below).

## In the audience

With Multiplayer → **Idle players join the audience** (on by default; it only
does anything in co-op), a player with no character in the battle (player 3
or 4 whose companion isn't the partner fighting, player 2 while the partner
is player 3's or 4's, anyone when no partner is out) gets a Toad of their own
at the front of the crowd, tinted and tagged in their colour ("2P", "3P",
"4P"). It isn't counted in the crowd, so Star Power and the crowd's size are
as they were.

- The stick moves it a little left or right.
- **A** cheer, **B** boo, **X** clap, **Y** jump, **D-pad up** wave, **D-pad
  down** laugh: the crowd's own animations, a speech bubble and a crowd
  sound. They change nothing in the battle; one each 40 frames at most.
- **Hold L** and aim with the stick (Mario, the partner, Luigi...), let go: a
  Mushroom (5 HP) for the one aimed at, or Honey Syrup (5 FP) when their HP
  is full. **Hold R** likewise: a rock at an enemy, 1 damage (never its last
  HP). **Z** puts it away. One throw a turn and three a battle, and only while
  a command is being chosen, never during an action (so never in an action
  command's timing). A throw that can't be made now is refused with a shrug;
  the tag is faint while there's no throw left this turn.

Online the battle's player 1's game decides: the idle player's game asks it,
and it sends what happens (the emote, the throw, then what the throw did) to
every game, so all screens show the same and the HP or FP is in its next
step's snapshot ([STATE_SYNC.md](STATE_SYNC.md#layer-3-battles-together)).
The crowd's own random throws stay off in a shared battle.

## With Brothers mode

With Multiplayer → **Brothers mode** on ([BROTHERS.md](BROTHERS.md)), player 2 plays Luigi instead of
the partner, and the others keep their parts:

| Player | In the field | In battle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mario | Mario's turns and guards, and the partner's |
| 2 | Luigi | Luigi's turns and guards |
| 3 | the follower, else a companion | the partner's, when it's their companion's partner |
| 4 | a companion | likewise |

The partner just follows Mario then ("2P" is over Luigi). Luigi and player
4's companion each have a place of their own beside the partner and the
follower, so all five can be out at once; neither is ever saved.

## The players' bars

When more than one plays (or online, or a controller is there to join), a row
of four bars runs along the bottom of the picture in a calm moment of the
field: 1P to 4P, each a quarter of the width, in every aspect ratio and on
every screen size. A player's bar is in their colour with their number, face
and character; a free seat's is a quiet one that says how to join (online,
"Open"), so nothing moves as players come and go. A player's notes are said in
their bar ("{START} for your menu" as they join, "1P has the menu", "Paused
by", a seat kept while a controller is away). A tap or click on your bar opens
your card, the same as your Start.

## Players' cards

Player 1's Start opens the game's full Start menu, which pauses everyone
(online, the host's pause is everyone's). Players 2-4's Start opens a small
menu of their own instead: a card that rises from their bar. Player 1 has
one too: a tap or click on 1P's bar, or Start while holding Z (Start alone
stays the Start menu); its Character strip is Mario's partners, and a pick
switches to them as the partner wheel does. Nothing pauses and the others play
on; their character stands still while it is open. Up and down (stick or
D-pad) choose, A does it, B goes back, Start closes it.

- **Your character:** another free partner, as L or R does (in Brothers mode
  player 2 is Luigi).
- **Who's who:** ask for another seat (on one computer the swap comes at the
  next calm moment; online its player says yes or no), or answer a request.
- **Emotes:** a bubble over their character: surprised, puzzled, thinking,
  dizzy, grumpy, happy.
- **Your controls:** their controller's buttons (online on a touch screen, the
  on-screen controls' layout too).
- **Your screen** (online): HUD size, message size, less screen shake, less
  flashing, for their own picture only.
- **Leave the room** (online, A twice).

There are no items here: the bag stays player 1's. A card closes by itself on a room change, a scene, a battle or player
1's Start menu. Online, a co-op guest's Start opens this menu in their own game
in place of the full Start menu. An emote travels in the player's controller
input, as a ping does: replays show it, and online the host's game shows a
guest's (`port/src/common/player_menu_pc.c`).

In the field, players 2-4 hold Z for a small wheel of the emotes: the stick
points, letting go shows it. Player 1 has the wheel too, but Z is Mario's as
well (pressed, the HUD), so theirs comes only after Z is held about a fifth of
a second alone, Mario standing, the stick at rest and nothing else pressed;
anything else in that time and that hold stays Mario's.

While player 1's Start menu is up, another player's Start shows "1P has the
menu" for a moment in their bar; the bars go to the top edge for it, since the
Start menu's help box is along the bottom (nothing else of the port's is drawn
over that menu; co-op notices wait until it closes). Online, a guest's screen
doesn't have the host's Start menu: while it is up the guest's bar shows
"Paused by" and the host's name until it closes.

Online, every screen draws every player's card at that player's place, as on
one computer: each game sends its own player's card (open, page, cursor,
the wheel, a swap asked of them, emotes) with its player message, and only
that player's controller moves it.

## Keeping a co-op adventure

Online, a guest's game keeps a copy of the host's adventure as the host's game
saves. On the guest's next launch the launcher's Play tab and the game's file
menu ask "Keep your co-op save with (the host)?": choose a file for it (a free
one; the launcher can also replace one in use after a second press, its save
staying in Save files), **Not now** (asked again next time) or **Don't ask
again**. The copy is a file of its own, played alone from there
(`port/src/common/coop_copy_pc.c`).

## On one computer

Press **Start** on a controller to join: it takes the next free seat (2P, then
3P, then 4P; the controller moves to that seat's port), and a note at that
player's place says so. A seated player's Start opens their own menu; an
unseated controller's Start never opens player 1's.

A controller is never a player only for being there. SDL puts every controller
that connects in the next free port: the first (a handheld's built-in controls,
the first controller paired with a phone or tablet) is player 1's, and the next
ones wait in theirs as nobody until their Start, whatever Multiplayer →
**Co-op (up to 4 players)** says. A player's seat can also be given on purpose:
Players lists all four players (who each plays, and their controller; online, a
guest's seat as "online: their name") and has **Choose player N's controller**
for players 2, 3 and 4 (any controller but the one player 1 plays on; while
player 1 plays on the keyboard or the touch screen, that one too; a controller
chosen from another seat leaves that seat), or a controller's player on
Controls → Controllers. **Let player N go** and **Not playing** free the seat at
once. A player who leaves takes their
companion away. Tests script controllers 2 to 4 with `TTYD_P2_PRESS`,
`TTYD_P3_PRESS` and `TTYD_P4_PRESS` ([PC_PORT.md](PC_PORT.md)), which play with
the setting on as a joined controller does, and plug in virtual controllers with
`TTYD_SEATS_TEST`; the log's `[coop]` lines say who joins, whose companion comes
and whether it's lent.

## Online

Friends join a room from **Online...** and take the next seats; who plays
what is **Who's who**. Online, everyone plays the host's adventure ([STATE_SYNC.md](STATE_SYNC.md#playing-together)):
each guest takes a seat, 2 (the partner, or Luigi with Brothers mode on), 3 or 4
(the follower or a companion), in the order they join, and their controller
plays that seat in the host's game as a controller on the host's computer
would. A controller plugged into the host's computer keeps its own seat, so two
on one computer and friends online mix. Any player can ask for another seat
(its holder answers), and the host can move anyone.

- Each guest's game shows the host's world: the same room, the host's
  characters as the host's game moves them, its scenes and text boxes.
- With **Camera** on Fit everyone (each player's own setting), a guest's
  screen follows the guest's own character, so they can explore the whole room
  out of Mario's view; the host's screen follows Mario (fitting only the
  players on the host's computer). A guest's character goes anywhere in the
  room: it comes back to Mario's side only when it falls, gets stuck or a scene
  starts, and every door still takes everyone along. Away from Mario it walks,
  hops, talks to whoever is in reach and hits blocks and switches; a story
  scene, a door, a pipe or the partner's field ability (player 2's X) is
  still Mario's, with everyone at his side.
- One who leaves frees their seat; their character follows Mario again (a
  companion goes). Nobody else's game ends for it.

## Co-op pings

Multiplayer → **Co-op pings** (on by default) lets players 1 and 2 mark a
spot or an enemy with a paper arrow both see, on one computer or online. Hold
**Ping** (Bindings) and aim with the C-stick, then let go; a quick press
marks the nearest enemy, else where the character stands. A click on the picture (a middle
click works over the menus too) or a tap marks that spot. An arrow near an
enemy goes on it and follows it, and stays five seconds. The ping travels in
the controller's input, so both games of an online session, replays and
watchers show the same arrows; the game itself sees nothing of it
(`port/src/common/ping_pc.c`, `port/settings/features/pings.cpp`).

## Saves and replays

A companion is never saved as the story's follower: a save made with one
loads without it. Replays keep all four controllers (`PCRPLAY2`) when
controllers 3 or 4 were there; older replays, and those without them, are
`PCRPLAY1` as before ([REPLAYS.md](REPLAYS.md)).

## For other features

`port/include/port/players.h`: `portCoopPlayers()` (1 to 4) and
`portCoopPlayerHere(player)`, from the frame's controllers as played (the

`portStateSyncBattleSelf()` and `portStateSyncPlayerName()` for online play's
own (`port/include/port/statesync.h`).
