# Full run, segment 16: from gon_06 just out of gon_07's spike trap (segment 15, 44) to the black chest
# (380 0 0): a first talk, then the Black Key from the list; it opens and the Paper Mage gives Mario the Paper
# Curse and his lesson: hold R until he has been paper for 180 frames (evt_lect_gon.c slit_majin_lecture;
# released early, the lesson waits for R again), and "no" to hearing it again (stg1_gon_12_15_yn: 1) ends it
# (45).
wait_seq 2 3000
press_until_control a 3000
where
walk_to 380 45 600               # the black chest
stick 0 100 12                   # pressed against its front
press a                          # it talks (once)
wait 30
press_until_control a 6000
where flag GSWF_GON06_BLACK_CHEST_TALKED
walk_to 380 100 60               # off and back against it
walk_to 380 40 60
stick 0 100 20
press a                          # the Black Key from the list: it opens, the Paper Mage
wait 30
press_until_control a 12000
where
hold r 300                       # his lesson: paper, held
wait 240
where
press_until_story a 45 3000      # the rest of his words (45)
press_until_control a 6000
where
where have 2
save
end
